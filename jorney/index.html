Who she is

A cat who learned early that the fastest way through a locked door is to be invited. Jorney of Hisynth left the marsh of her birth for the cities, where a quick smile, quicker hands and an ear for secrets earned her a living — and a few enemies who still don't know her real name. Her older sister Mist stayed behind. Jorney writes when she's in trouble. She's usually in trouble.

The miniature: calico face and tail, eyes that glow green in the dark. Hand-painted by Jason Lutz for Josie, who built the character.
