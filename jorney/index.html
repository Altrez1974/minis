Who she is

A cat who learned early that the fastest way through a locked door is to be invited. Jorney of Hisynth left the marsh of her birth for the cities, where a quick smile, quicker hands and an ear for secrets made her the one you want on your side — and the one your enemies never see coming. Her older sister Mist stayed behind in the marsh. The two write often. When one of them is in trouble, the other is already on the road.

The miniature: calico face and tail, eyes that glow green in the dark. Hand-painted by Jason Lutz for Josie, who built the character.
